Pre-Production Workflow

FROM ROUGH IDEA TO SHOOT-READY IN FOUR STEPS.

boardie keeps visual prep calm and structured; whether you are blocking scenes for a short film or gathering lighting and styling references for a portrait shoot.

Step-by-Step Guide

HOW YOUR STUDIO FLOWS

Step 01 Project Setup

CREATE A PROJECT BOARD

Sign up or log in to enter your workspace. Start a dedicated Project Board for each film, music video, campaign, or editorial shoot so nothing gets mixed together.

Step 02 Visual Framing

ADD SHOT CARDS & MOOD PALETTE CARDS

Drop in Shot Cards with your target aspect ratio (16:9, 2.39:1, 4:5, or 9:16), lens notes, and camera movement - or build Mood Cards with color swatches and lighting references.

Step 03 Structure

NEST SUB-BOARDS & PRODUCTION NOTES

Break complex shoots into focused Sub-Boards for specific scenes, locations, or wardrobe looks, and pin Production Notes for call-sheet reminders, gear checks, and crew cues.

Step 04 On Set

REVIEW & SHOOT WITH CLARITY

Everything stays saved in your workspace so you can check framing ratios, color palettes, and scene notes on set without digging through loose files or chat threads.
