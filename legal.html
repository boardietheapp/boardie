02. PRIVACY POLICY

Data & Storage

We collect only the information needed to run your account, sync your boards and projects, send requested updates or newsletters, and process subscription billing. We do not sell your personal data or creative boards to third parties.

When using local browser storage or read-only preview links, remember that shared links expose snapshot board data to anyone who has the URL.

03. SUBSCRIPTIONS, PAYMENTS & REFUNDS

Paystack Billing

Paid plans renew according to your chosen billing cycle (monthly or annual) unless canceled prior to renewal. Payments are processed through supported payment channels such as Paystack; boardie does not store raw card credentials directly on our servers.

If you experience a duplicate charge or billing error, reach out promptly so we can review and resolve it.
